01 / the person behind the pages

A little about me

Somewhere between art, words, leadership, and daydreams — that's where you'll find me.

“Creativity is how I make sense of the world.”

I'm a HUMSS student with a background in the Special Program in the Arts (Visual Arts). I enjoy expressing ideas through different forms — from illustrations and photographs to writing, performance, and handmade creations.

My experiences as a student journalist, class leader, event host, and performer have taught me to be brave with my ideas and thoughtful with my voice.

My little timeline
